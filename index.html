01 / RESEARCH

The physics between
light and function.

New semiconductor materials offer new possibilities for harvesting light. We investigate what happens in the moments after they absorb it, and why those early events matter for performance.

01 / MATERIALS

Emerging absorbers

From metal-halide perovskites to perovskite-inspired and chalcohalide systems, we look for the material properties behind useful optoelectronic behavior.

↗
02 / DYNAMICS

Charge in motion

We follow how photoexcited carriers move, localize, and recombine across ultrafast timescales.

↗
03 / INTERFACES

Light meets matter

We explore how structure, interfaces, and optical environments shape the pathways available to excited states.

02 / APPROACH

A picture measured
